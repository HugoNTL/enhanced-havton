Prompt-generation templates

DreamWaltz

Inspect the paired person and garment images. Write one concise, standalone prompt beginning with “A” that directly describes the final avatar already wearing the garment. Include the person’s visible facial features, skin tone, body shape, pose, hair, remaining clothing and accessories, plus the garment’s category, color, pattern, material, neckline, sleeves, length, fit, details, and natural drape. Never mention images, references, preservation, replacement, editing, and do not invent obscured details.

Outpainting

For every image, write a short, image-specific outpainting prompt that extends the visible half-body portrait into a natural, coherent full-body image. Preserve the person’s identity, pose, clothing style, proportions, lighting, viewpoint, and background; infer plausible lower-body clothing, footwear, and leg position without changing visible details.

Both prompt templates and sample prompts for DreamWaltz and Outpainting were generated by ChatGPT.
